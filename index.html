About Us

We are a multi‑service building installation company specializing in electrical wiring, plumbing systems, CCTV surveillance, and fire‑fighting safety solutions. Our goal is to deliver safe, durable, and cost‑effective installations for residential, commercial, and industrial buildings.

Core Services

Residential Electrical Services

  • Wiring and rewiring of homes
  • Electrical panel upgrades
  • Lighting installation (interior and exterior)
  • Electrical outlets and switches installation
  • Home automation systems
  • Electrical troubleshooting and repairs
  • Smoke detector and security system installation

Commercial Electrical Services

  • Installation and maintenance of lighting, outlets, and electrical panels
  • Electrical systems design for commercial buildings
  • Energy-efficient solutions (LED lighting, solar installations)
  • Wiring for HVAC systems, refrigeration, and equipment
  • Electrical upgrades for new construction or renovations
  • Electrical inspections and compliance with codes

Fire Fitting & Safety Systems

  • Fire alarm system installation
  • Smoke & heat detectors
  • Fire extinguisher setup & sprinklers
  • Emergency exit lighting
  • Fire safety audits & compliance
  • Professional firefighting equipment installation
  • Fire safety training and consultation

CCTV & Security Systems

  • Indoor & outdoor CCTV installation
  • DVR/NVR setup & mobile monitoring
  • Night-vision & motion-detection cameras
  • Access control & biometric systems
  • Annual maintenance contracts
  • Professional camera fitting and calibration
  • 24/7 surveillance system monitoring
