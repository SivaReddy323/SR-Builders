About Us

We are a multi-service building installation company specializing in electrical wiring, plumbing systems, CCTV camera fitting, sensor door lock installation, and fire safety solutions. Our goal is to deliver safe, durable, and cost-effective installations for residential, commercial, and industrial buildings.

Krishna Enterprises is a well-established contracting firm specializing in electrical and plumbing material contract services since 2003. With over 20 years of industry experience, we have become a trusted partner for builders, developers, and infrastructure companies across major cities in India. Backed by a dedicated team of professionals, we deliver high-quality electrical and plumbing solutions for residential, commercial, industrial, and infrastructure projects, ensuring safe, efficient, and durable installations that meet industry standards and exceed client expectations.
